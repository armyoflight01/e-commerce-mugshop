Slow Craft in a Fast World

Each mug in our collection starts as an unformed lump of raw mineral stoneware clay. Centered on the potter’s wheel, it is thrown, shaped, trimmed, and fitted with an ergonomic handle contoured for human fingers.

After slow air-drying, our mugs undergo two kiln firings: a bisque fire at 950°C and a final high-fire glaze at 1250°C that vitrifies the stoneware, rendering it non-porous and microwave safe.

Mineral Glazes

Natural earth oxides create subtle speckles and organic shade transitions without harmful chemicals.

Thermal Density

Thick ceramic walls retain heat significantly longer than commercial factory pressed cups.

Plastic-Free
